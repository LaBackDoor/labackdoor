We study how machine learning and large language models can strengthen security — and how
they fail. Work spans byte-level models, agentic AI systems, and representation learning for
security tasks.

## AI Safety Discourse

Alongside the technical work, Abanisenioluwa is a graduate research fellow at **BASE Research**
(from October 2026). The project uses computational text analysis to study how the AI safety
field defines and prioritizes risk, whose perspectives shape the discussion, and which voices
and countries are underrepresented. It compares catastrophic-risk framings, including loss of
control, with present-day concerns such as bias, misuse and reliability, using a corpus of
academic papers, community discussions, safety-organization publications and summit documents.
The analysis combines embeddings, topic modeling and LLM-assisted classification checked
against human-coded examples, and tracks changes in risk framing and citation patterns over
time. The aim is to surface blind spots in the conversations that shape AI research
priorities, funding and governance.
