Typed decision models answer fixed questions about a serialized record, such as whether a
network flow is malicious and which attack class it belongs to, in one non-autoregressive
pass with a confidence attached and output that always conforms to a declared schema. The
paper examines how calibration collapses and how adversarial state injection can manipulate
such models when they are used for intrusion detection and alert triage.
